Animation

Changed since this recording. The video still shows what was recorded; Re-record to film it with the changes.

Scroll and Path changes are what plays from now on (in ⌘. and ⌘T too) and reach the video when you re-record. Trim and the cursor are applied when saving.

Cursor

The cursor shows inside the spans on the Cursor track, fading in and out at each edge. Drag on the track to add one, drag a span to move it or its edges to resize it; ⌫ removes the selected span.

There's no cursor data for this video, so there's no cursor to add. Recordings made with this version of Darc have it.

Trim

Drag the yellow handles on the Video track, or press I and O to start and end at the playhead. Drag across the Video track to pick a stretch (a pause, a page loading) and ⌫ cuts it out; everything after closes up, the animation and cursor with it. Click a red seam and ⌫ to put it back.
