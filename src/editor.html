Cursor

The cursor shows inside the spans on the Cursor track, fading in and out at each edge. Drag on the track to add one, drag a span to move it or its edges to resize it; ⌫ removes the selected span.

There's no cursor data for this video, so there's no cursor to add. Recordings made with this version of Darc have it.
